Cash Memer Announces Its Trademark Portfolio

A brand-protection initiative and a published list of the marks that identify Cash Memer

CTCash Memer Team Jul 18, 2026 1 min read

On this page

Respecting the marksAbout Cash Memer

Cash Memer today announced the trademarks and brand identifiers owned and used by the company as part of its ongoing commitment to protecting its intellectual property and maintaining a consistent brand identity.

Our trademarks represent the products, services, logos, names and visual elements that distinguish the Cash Memer platform. These assets are an important part of our brand and are protected under applicable trademark laws.

The list of trademarks may be updated from time to time as new products, features, services and branding are introduced. You can view the full list — each mark with a short description — on the Cash Memer Trademarks page.

Respecting the marks

Cash Memer requests that all individuals, organizations, developers, partners, media outlets and third parties respect these trademark rights. The use of Cash Memer trademarks must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership or affiliation unless expressly authorized in writing.

Additional trademarks, logos, slogans and product names may be added as the Cash Memer ecosystem continues to expand.

For trademark-related inquiries, licensing requests or questions regarding permitted use, please contact the Cash Memer Legal team through the official support channels.

About Cash Memer

Cash Memer is a receipt and expense management platform designed to help users organize purchases, manage financial records and simplify everyday expense tracking through modern technology and intelligent automation. See the full list of protected marks on the Cash Memer Trademarks page.
