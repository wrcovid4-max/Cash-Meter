Cash Memer Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 14, 2026

Cash Memer ("Cash Memer," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

By downloading, installing, or using Cash Memer, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Depending on the features you choose to use, Cash Memer may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your Google account information (such as your name, email address, and profile photo), receipts, receipt images, product information, customer information, notes, signatures, backup data, application settings, and other information that you choose to save within the application.

When you use AI Receipt Scanner, selected receipt images are securely transmitted to Google's Gemini AI service solely for the purpose of extracting receipt information. The extracted information is returned to Cash Memer for your review. You remain responsible for verifying the accuracy of AI-generated results before saving them.

If you enable Cloud Backup & Sync, your selected application data may be securely stored using Google Firebase services. Cloud synchronization is optional and may be disabled at any time.

If you choose to tag receipts with your current location, Cash Memer will request permission to access your device's location. Location information is collected only when you explicitly request it and is not used for continuous tracking.

Camera permission is used solely for scanning receipts and barcodes. Photo library access is used only to allow you to import receipt images. NFC permission is used only when you choose to scan supported NFC tags.

Internet access is required for features including AI Receipt Scanner, Cloud Backup & Sync, Google Sign-In, live currency exchange rates, weather information, and other online services.

Cash Memer's website does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. We do not place cookies on your device for analytics, advertising, personalization, marketing, or visitor tracking. You can browse our website without accepting or managing cookies.

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information. Information is shared only when necessary to provide the services you request through trusted third-party providers, including Google Sign-In, Firebase, Gemini AI, ExchangeRate-API, and Open-Meteo.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You may delete receipts, products, backups, or other stored information at any time. You may disable cloud synchronization, sign out of your Google account, delete local application data, or uninstall the application whenever you choose.

Cash Memer is not directed toward children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your information may be processed or stored in countries where our trusted third-party service providers operate. By using Cash Memer, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in accordance with applicable laws.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will become effective upon publication of the revised Privacy Policy, together with an updated Effective Date.
