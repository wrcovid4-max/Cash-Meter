This policy explains what data the Cash Memer app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, Android and Wear OS versions. Cash Memer is published by [your name or company] (“we”, “us”). Questions go to support@cashmemer.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier. These identify your account and let your data restore on another device.
  • Receipts and cash memos. Everything you enter or scan — store name, items, quantities, prices, tax, discount, currency, category, payment type, notes, customer name, phone and email, member records and your digital signature.
  • Products and members. Your product catalogue, including barcodes and categories, and the customers saved in your members directory.
  • Location. If you use the GPS button on a receipt, the app reads your device location to fill in the store address. Location is used only for that field, only when you ask for it.
  • Photos and camera. Receipt images you capture or import for scanning, and barcode scans.
  • App settings. Language, theme, currencies, print preferences, and whether app lock is enabled.
  • Diagnostics. [Name any crash reporting or analytics you use — for example Firebase Crashlytics — and what it collects. If you collect none, say so here instead.]

What we don't collect

  • No card numbers or bank credentials. The app records which payment method you chose; it does not process payments.
  • No contacts, and no continuous or background location tracking.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Receipt scanning (Gemini)

When you scan or import a receipt, the image is sent to Google's Gemini service, which reads it and returns the parsed fields that populate the form. The image and the text extracted from it are processed for that purpose. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. If you'd rather not send an image off the device, enter the receipt manually — every field the scanner fills can be typed in by hand.

Exchange rates and weather

Currency rates come from ExchangeRate-API, all valued against 1 US Dollar. Weather is fetched for your general area to show conditions on the dashboard and watch. These requests carry only what's needed to answer them — currency codes and an approximate location. They do not include your receipts, your totals or your account details. Liability for incorrect rate information lies with the rate provider.

Google Sign-In and cloud sync

Sign-in is handled by Google; we never see your Google password. If you enable cloud sync, your receipts and drafts are stored in Firebase in the [region] region, protected by your account credentials and encrypted in transit with TLS. You can disconnect the app at any time from your Google account permissions page, and sign out from within the app.

Where your data lives

Receipts are written to a local database on your device first, so the app works offline. A copy goes to your cloud store only if you turn sync on. You can also export your own backups at any time — Settings → Backup & Recovery writes a JSON file or the raw SQLite database to Drive or local storage, or copies your records to the clipboard. Those exports are yours; we never receive them.

How long we keep it

Synced data is retained while your account is active. Deleting the app removes everything held on that device, and “Delete All Receipts” in settings clears your records. To delete synced account data, use the in-app option or email support@cashmemer.com; we action deletion requests within [30] days.
