What's inside

Everything a cash memo needs

From the moment you point the camera at a paper receipt to the moment the printed memo lands in the customer's hand — it's all in one app.

✨

AI OCR scanner (Gemini)

Photograph any paper receipt or pick one from the gallery and Gemini parses it, auto-populating every form field. Bulk scan queues a whole stack of images and saves them in one go.

🏷️

Barcode scanning

Scan a barcode with the camera, an external professional scanner, or NFC. Matched products drop straight onto the memo with quantity and price.

🧾

The memo builder

Store name, GPS address, member, customer contact, currency, category and payment type — Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, Mobile Wallet, Apple Pay, Google Wallet, Google Pay, Klarna or PayPak. Items, tax, cash given and change are worked out for you.

✍️

Digital signature

Sign the memo on the glass, or with Apple Pencil on iPad. Save a default signature so it's applied automatically on every receipt you generate.

📄

Two-page PDF viewer

Pan, annotate with text, stamp a tick or a cross, undo and redo. Search, share, print, or turn on auto-print and mass print for page one, page two, or both.

💱

Live exchange rates

Every currency valued against 1 USD, powered by ExchangeRate-API, with search, one-tap refresh and a visible last-updated stamp. Register your own custom currency if yours isn't listed.

📦

Products & members

A product catalogue with barcodes, categories and archive/duplicate actions, plus a members directory so a regular customer's details fill themselves in.

📊

Dashboard & AI insights

Sales today, income today, total products, scans today and API status at a glance — plus a Weekly AI Summary that reads your history back to you as business insights.

☁️

Google Sign-In & cloud sync

Sign in with Google and your receipts and drafts stay synced through Firebase. Switch devices, or restore everything after a reset, without losing a single memo.

💾

Offline backup

Save a JSON backup to Drive or local storage, export the raw SQLite database, or copy records to the clipboard. Restore any of them instantly.

🔒

App lock

Require a PIN, password, pattern, fingerprint or face unlock on launch and when returning from the background, with a Material 3 dialer and a 4-digit passcode fallback.

🌤️

Weather & glance

Local conditions, humidity, wind and rainfall sit at the top of the screen — and on the watch, so a glance at your wrist shows the weather, the rates and your history.

Common questions

Which platforms is Cash Memer on?

iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, Android and Wear OS today. visionOS, Android XR, CarPlay and Android Auto are in development. All features are available regardless of platform, though some may not be available in every region or language.

How does the receipt scanner work?

Take a picture of a paper receipt, or pick one from your gallery, and Gemini parses it and auto-populates the form fields for you. Bulk scan handles a queue of images in one pass, and you can scan a barcode with the camera, an external scanner or NFC instead.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

From ExchangeRate-API, with all currencies valued against 1 US Dollar. Rates are shown with the time they were last updated. Liability for any incorrect rate information lies with the rate provider.

What if I lose my phone or reset it?

If you're signed in with Google, your receipts and drafts restore from Firebase on the new device. We'd also recommend keeping an offline backup — Settings → Backup & Recovery exports a JSON file or the raw SQLite database to Drive or local storage.

Can I use it in Urdu?

Yes — the entire app, including the printed cash memo and the watch apps, runs in Urdu with a right-to-left layout. Switch with the ENG / اردو control in the app header at any time.

Does it have dark mode?

System-wide dark mode, light mode and an English-to-Urdu option are all available, set under Appearance in settings. This website follows the same three choices.

Is my data private?

Your receipts live on your device, and in your own Google-backed cloud store if you enable sync. See the privacy policy for the details.
